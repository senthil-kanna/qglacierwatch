## Geographic alignment

- Outlet in config (77.146 E, 31.877 N) is approximate for Bhuntar and should be replaced by the exact station coordinates.
- The GloFAS cell (77.125 E, 31.825 N) was chosen on the Beas main stem just below the Parvati confluence so its upstream area matches the traced catchment. GloFAS upstream area for that cell was not retrieved here and should be compared with 3,532 km2.
- Basin means are computed over the traced polygon at 500 m (MODIS) and ~11 km (ERA5-Land). ERA5-Land cells straddle the boundary.

## Known data issues handled in code

- ERA5-Land accumulations contain values down to -1.5e-5 mm; clipped to 0 (`merge_daily`).
- ERA5-Land basin-mean SWE never drops below ~1,360 mm because glacier grid cells carry a fixed deep snowpack; only 30-day SWE change is used.
- MODIS cloud gaps: snow fraction is forward-filled causally with an explicit `snow_age` feature; no interpolation across future values.
- GloFAS shows almost no April-June rise at this cell (monthly means 41-49 m3/s Jan-Jun, 245 m3/s in August). This looks like a snowmelt-timing bias and limits what skill against GloFAS means.

## Units

Discharge m3/s; temperature degC (converted from K); precipitation, snowfall, snowmelt, SWE mm (converted from m); snow fractions 0-1; lake areas km2.
